Music on tanadol-l.vercel.app
=============================

Pieces are performed live in the browser from MIDI files, on a sampled piano.

MIDI files: The Mutopia Project (https://www.mutopiaproject.org)
  - J.S. Bach, Prelude in C major BWV 846 ............ Public Domain
  - J.S. Bach, Goldberg Variations: Aria BWV 988 ..... CC BY-SA 3.0
  - C. Debussy, Clair de Lune ........................ Public Domain
  - F. Chopin, Nocturne Op. 9 No. 2 .................. CC BY-SA 3.0
  - L. van Beethoven, Für Elise WoO 59 ............... Public Domain
  - C. Debussy, Première Arabesque ................... Public Domain

Piano samples (public/piano/): Salamander Grand Piano, Yamaha C5,
  by Alexander Holm, CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/),
  via https://github.com/Tonejs/audio
